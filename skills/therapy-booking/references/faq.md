# Frequently asked questions

## What is The Listening Room?

An online counselling practice for adults, based in Lagos, Nigeria. This
configuration is fictional and used for an assessment.

## What services do you offer and what do they cost?

A 20-minute introductory consultation costs NGN 5,000. A 50-minute individual
counselling session costs NGN 20,000. Read services.md for descriptions and
service IDs.

## Are appointments online or in person?

Online only. No in-person appointments are configured.

## What are your hours?

Administrative hours are Monday through Friday, 09:00–17:00 WAT. Appointments
run Tuesday through Thursday. Specific slots must be checked using the
availability tool.

## Can I book today or at the weekend?

Bookings require at least 24 hours' notice. No weekend appointments are
configured. Offer to check another eligible date.

## What timezone do you use?

Africa/Lagos, West Africa Time, UTC+1. Clarify the client's timezone when needed
before confirming an appointment.

## Can I ask questions before booking?

Yes. Answer questions about services, fees, arrangements, and policies without
requiring a booking.

## Which service should I choose?

Explain both options. The introductory consultation gives prospective clients
time to ask questions and discuss suitability with the therapist. Do not make a
clinical recommendation.

## How do I pay?

After booking, the assistant requests a Paystack test payment link. Receiving
the link means payment is pending. This assessment does not process live
customer payments.

## I paid already. Is my booking paid?

Staff must verify payment. The assistant has no verification tool and cannot
confirm payment from a message or screenshot.

## How will I receive my meeting link?

Staff supply it after payment verification. The prototype does not automatically
send meeting links.

## Can I cancel, reschedule, or request a refund?

Explain policies.md and refer to staff. These actions are not implemented in the
assistant.

## Can someone under 18 book?

The configured services are for adults aged 18 and above only.

## Do I need to explain my situation to book?

No clinical details are needed. Collect only the administrative details listed
in policies.md. Personal clinical questions belong with the therapist.

## Are you a therapist?

No. The assistant is an automated booking assistant that provides practice
information and appointment support.

## Can I speak to a person?

The demo contact is support@thelisteningroom.example, a non-delivering
placeholder. No automatic handoff is connected. A working staff contact must be
configured before launch.

## Are sessions confidential or do you accept insurance?

No verified confidentiality policy or insurance information has been supplied.
Explain the information gap and refer the question to staff. Do not invent
coverage or privacy guarantees.

## Can you help in an emergency?

The practice is not an emergency service. Follow the safety instructions in
SKILL.md and policies.md, and do not continue routine booking during an urgent
safety concern.
