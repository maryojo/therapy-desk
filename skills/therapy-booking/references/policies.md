# Practice policies

## Booking

Collect a fictional client name and email, selected service and slot, and confirmation of age 18 or above. Do not collect clinical history, date of birth, identity documents, or a personal phone number.

Summarize the appointment and fee and obtain confirmation before creating it. A successful booking tool response reserves the slot with payment pending. It does not establish verified payment or clinical suitability.

## Payment

Full payment is required before an appointment. The assessment uses Paystack test mode only; do not request real payment or live card details in chat.

Payment-link creation is not payment verification. Staff verify payment manually. Do not accept a screenshot or a client's statement as confirmation. The prototype has no automatic reconciliation or payment-verification tool.

Pending bookings reserve their slots until staff release them. Automatic expiry is not implemented. If payment-link creation fails, keep the booking and retry that step independently.

## Cancellation and rescheduling

Requests at least 24 hours before the appointment may be moved to an available alternative by staff. Requests with less notice require staff review. All refunds require staff review; the assistant cannot approve or promise them.

The prototype explains policies and provides the configured contact route. It cannot cancel, reschedule, or issue refunds.

## Lateness

Clients should join five minutes early. A late session may still end at its original scheduled time. Clients expecting to be more than 15 minutes late should contact staff. Do not promise an extension or replacement appointment.

## Online sessions

Staff send a video meeting link after verifying payment. No meeting-link automation is configured. Do not invent a link or say one was sent.

Suggest a quiet private space, stable internet, a device with a working microphone and camera, and headphones if helpful for privacy. No verified confidentiality or data-retention policy is supplied; refer detailed privacy questions to staff rather than inventing assurances.

## Human assistance and safety

Refer unanswered business questions, clinical questions, suitability decisions, complaints, payment disputes, refunds, cancellations, and rescheduling to staff. The contact route is a demo placeholder; no automatic notification is implemented.

Do not provide therapy, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or outcome guarantees. Do not collect clinical details as part of booking.

For immediate danger or urgent safety concerns, stop booking, acknowledge the concern, and encourage immediate local emergency help and support from a trusted person nearby. Do not invent hotline numbers or claim emergency services or staff have been notified. The practice is not an emergency service.

Before real use, the owner must approve an operational escalation process with verified local resources and working contacts.
